Vector Rally

You do not steer the car. You steer its acceleration.

How it works
  • Your car has a position and a velocity. Each turn you change the velocity by −1, 0 or +1 in each direction — nine choices — and the car moves by the new velocity.
  • Neither part of the velocity may go outside −5…5.
  • Everything the car passes over counts: walls, other cars, checkpoints and the finish line. At speed 5 you cannot jump over any of them.
  • Leave the track and the car is put back on the last cell it reached, with its speed reset to zero. That costs you the turn.
  • A lap counts when you cross the finish line the right way round with every checkpoint collected in order.

On a keyboard: 1–9 (or the numpad) pick one of the nine moves, laid out the way the numpad is. Space confirms, U undoes, O zooms out to the whole track.

On a touch screen: hold a button on the pad to see where the move goes, and let go to take it. Sliding your finger off the button cancels. You can also touch one of the nine marked cells on the track itself.

Install it: on Android, Chrome's ⋮ menu → “Add to Home screen”. On iOS, the share button in Safari's toolbar → scroll down → “Add to Home Screen”. Load the game once with a network connection and it works offline afterwards.
