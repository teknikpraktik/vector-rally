Vector Rally

You do not steer the car. You steer its acceleration.

Track

Twelve tracks named after places that race cars have been driven round. They are drawn by hand on a grid about eighty cells wide, which is nowhere near enough to reproduce a real circuit — a hairpin comes out as a single cell. What they are after is the character of the place: Monza fast and barely interrupted, Monaco all corners. Treat them as sketches, not as maps.

Players

One lap, everybody on the line together.

How it works
  • Your car has a position and a velocity. Each turn you change the velocity by −1, 0 or +1 in each direction — nine choices — and the car then moves by the whole new velocity.
  • Neither part of the velocity may go outside −5…5.
  • Work out where that puts you. The game will not show you: adding the velocity to the position is the part worth practising. The pad tells you what the velocity becomes, and nothing else.
  • Everything the car passes over counts: walls, other cars, checkpoints and the finish line. At speed 5 you cannot jump over any of them.
  • Leave the track and the car is put back on the last cell it reached, with its speed reset to zero. That costs you the turn — so count carefully.
  • A move that is genuinely impossible — another car in the way, or over the speed limit — is crossed out on the pad and cannot be chosen.
  • One lap. It counts when you cross the line the way the arrows point, with every checkpoint collected in order.

The edge of the track is drawn as a smooth line, but the game is played on the grid underneath it. A cell is on the track when its corner falls inside the line.

On a keyboard: 1–9 (or the numpad) pick one of the nine moves, laid out the way the numpad is. Space confirms, U undoes, O zooms out to the whole track.

On a touch screen: press a button on the pad and let go to take that move. Sliding your finger off the button before letting go cancels it.

Install it: on Android, Chrome's ⋮ menu → “Add to Home screen”. On iOS, the share button in Safari's toolbar → scroll down → “Add to Home Screen”. Load the game once with a network connection and it works offline afterwards.
